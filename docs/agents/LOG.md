Train env metrics land as `env/all/<key>` and `env/<tag>/<key>` from `logging_tags()`; the built-in reward key is `env/all/reward/total`, plus `turns_per_episode`, `ac_tokens_per_turn`, `ob_tokens_per_turn`; anything the `reward_fn` returns in its metrics dict is mean-aggregated under the same prefix, so our `format_ok`, `citations_grounded`, `correctness`, `efficiency`, `tool_calls`, `prompt_tokens` keys come from the grader for free.
- Detail (NaN policy, gap_specs §4): total reward = sum of per-step rewards + `compute_group_rewards` return, so a NaN step reward poisons the sum. Plan: the env reward_fn returns 0.0 with metric `judge_error=1` on judge failure; `compute_group_rewards(trajectory_group, env_group)` then adds the mean of the non-error siblings' rewards to those trajectories, giving exactly the group mean and advantage 0. Groups where every sample errored get 0 everywhere and are dropped by `remove_constant_reward_groups=True`. `group_reward_std` and `unique_tool_sequences_per_group` also go in that hook's metrics dict.
- Detail (termination): `Config.termination=None` resolves to no reward clamp for Qwen models (only Inkling models get grade-then-clamp), so budget/max_turns stops keep our own grader's verdict. `build_agent_tool_env(max_tool_calls=...)` is checked pre-dispatch; env metrics `max_turns` and `max_tool_calls` are emitted on those stops.
- Affects: trainer, evals, agent (make_cookbook_env signature)

## 2026-09-19 12:00 · tinker client · product agent — handoff to C: what the product fixed, what training should change
- Two client failure modes seen tonight, both on a long-lived Tinker client, both invisible to the SDK's own retries:
  1. **Dead session**: the server ends a session whose heartbeats lapsed under congestion (`400 ... "This ServiceClient has finished (interrupted) and cannot run further operations"`; also seen as `"was cancelled"`). Every later call fails instantly. Product fix (`apps/api/server.py`): recognise the 400, `service_client.cache_clear()` + `sampling_client.cache_clear()`, re-run the episode once on a fresh session.
  2. **Wedged sampling client**: after a congested hour the SDK's per-sampling-client `SessionFuturesPoller` hangs every new sample until `CHAT_TIMEOUT`, while a fresh client answers in 1 s (23:24-23:47; the 9B client in the same session was fine: the poller is per client, and it keeps every abandoned request id, since `sample_cancel_enabled=False` makes client cancels a no-op). Product fix: before a Tinker ask, if the profile has not completed a call in 120 s, a 1-token probe with a 20 s timeout; on a hang, drop that client + `sampling_client.cache_clear()`, keep the session. 23 API tests pass.
- For training (C's files; not edited by me):
  a. `codeqa/trainer/config.py`: `RetryOnFailure(max_retries=8, per_rollout_timeout=1200)` amplifies congestion: a timed-out rollout's request keeps computing (cancel is off for the org) and the retry adds a second one; 8 retries × 16 groups can double an arm's load in the pool exactly when it is slow. Suggest `MinViableGroup(min_successful=spec.group_size - 2, max_retries=2, per_rollout_timeout=1200)`: partial groups are safe (pairing is trajectory-own per `test_pairing.py`; `compute_group_rewards` only needs `len(env_group) == len(trajectory_group)`).

  d. The per-step fresh sampling client already bounds mode 2 in training; nothing to do there.
